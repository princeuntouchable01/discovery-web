About These Games

Learn more about the titles featured on our hub

Call of Duty: Mobile

Developed by TiMi Studios and published by Activision (now part of Microsoft), Call of Duty: Mobile brings console-quality FPS action to your phone. It features multiple modes including classic Multiplayer, Battle Royale, Zombies, and DMZ: Recon. With seasonal updates, a World Championship esports scene, and deep weapon customization through Gunsmith, it remains one of the most polished shooters on mobile.

FPS / Battle Royale

Blood Strike

Developed by NetEase, this fast-paced tactical FPS delivers smooth aiming, responsive controls, and optimized performance even on lower-end devices. It offers a fresh take on battle royale with unique Striker abilities, customizable weapon loadouts, faction-based modes, and aerial movement options like jetpacks.

Tactical FPS

PUBG Mobile

Co-developed by LIGHTSPEED STUDIOS of Tencent Games and KRAFTON, Inc. Up to 100 players parachute onto massive maps like Erangel and Miramar, scavenging for weapons and gear. Features include Classic BR, Arena modes, Payload, Metro Royale, and monthly collaboration events.

Battle Royale / FPS

Standoff 2

Developed by Axlebolt, founded in 2015 in Limassol, Cyprus, with a global team of almost 300. This competitive FPS draws heavy inspiration from Counter-Strike, offering bomb-defusal and team deathmatch modes. It features a robust ranked system, weapon skins marketplace, clan wars, and regular esports tournaments.

Competitive FPS

eFootball

Developed and published by Konami, this free-to-play football simulation lets you build your Dream Team with real-world players and clubs. Features include Master League, online PvP divisions, co-op matches, and Smart Assist controls. With regular season updates, licensed kits, and cross-platform play between mobile and console.

Football / Simulation

NBA 2K Mobile

Developed by Cat Daddy Games (a 2K-owned studio under Take-Two Interactive), NBA 2K Mobile brings authentic basketball action with over 400 NBA players past and present. Build your MyTEAM, compete in online leagues, participate in limited-time events, and collect themed cards with console-quality graphics.

Basketball / Card Collector

Real Cricket 24

Developed by Nautilus Mobile, this is the most comprehensive cricket simulation on mobile, featuring real international stadiums, dynamic commentary, and authentic batting and bowling mechanics. Play Test matches, ODIs, T20s, and the World Cup across multiple difficulty levels with multiplayer support.

Cricket / Simulation

Garena Free Fire

Developed by 111 Dots Studio and published by Garena. One of the world's most popular mobile battle royales, Free Fire drops up to 52 players onto an island for 10-minute matches. Beyond BR, it offers Clash Squad (4v4), Lone Wolf, and over 15 rotating event modes with 50+ unique characters and special abilities.

Battle Royale

Roblox

Developed by Roblox Corporation, founded in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel. Headquartered in San Mateo, California with a team exceeding 2,400 employees. Roblox isn't just a game—it's a global platform powering millions of user-created experiences. With cross-platform play, avatar customization, voice chat, and a thriving creator economy.

Platform / UGC

Minecraft

Developed by Mojang Studios (a Microsoft subsidiary). Originally created by Markus "Notch" Persson. Current Studio Head: Kayleen Walters. Chief Creative Officer: Jens Bergensten. Game Director: Agnes Larsson. The legendary sandbox lets players mine, craft, build, and survive in procedurally generated block worlds with constant updates and infinite modding possibilities.

Sandbox / Survival

Race Max Pro

Developed by Revani, built for racing enthusiasts. Race Max Pro offers three distinct disciplines: Street Racing, Drifting, and Drag Racing. Featuring 50+ officially licensed vehicles from brands like BMW, Nissan, Pagani, and Ford, with deep car customization and online ranked leagues.

Racing / Simulation
